# 阶段英文回复范例与纠偏验收案例

本文件提供经人工确认的各阶段英文回复标准写法，以及历史会话中暴露过的典型偏差与纠正方式，供起草与自检时对照。

---

## 一、经确认的阶段标准范例

### 范例 1：第二轮多问题尽调答疑（Amir Aghsami 确认定稿，最新纯 URL 与结尾规范）

**场景**：已向专家发送 `Qiming_Program_Information.pdf`，专家回信询问：① 是否有官方政策文件、② 远程合作能否拿 100 万个人补贴、③ 是否收费、④ 能否保留教职与独立发表论文、⑤ 申报前能否先拿到企业信息。

```text
Dear Dr Aghsami,

Thank you for reviewing the program overview and sharing your questions.

1. Official documentation. The Qiming Program is not publicly promoted. We have the official policy documents in Chinese, but we cannot send copies. If you would like to discuss the program further, we can show you the documents during a video call. For background on the Qiming Program, see the Wikipedia article below.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thousand_Talents_Plan

2. Personal subsidy. Scholars who collaborate primarily remotely can be considered for the RMB 1 million personal salary subsidy, subject to a successful application and the applicable program conditions. This subsidy is separate from the project budget. The working arrangement mainly affects how project funding is allocated.

3. Fees. Qingfei does not charge experts application, administrative, or intermediary fees at any stage.

4. Academic position and publications. You can retain your academic position and continue your independent research and publications. Intellectual property, confidentiality, and publication rights for any joint project would be discussed with the company before you make a commitment.

5. Company information before applying. Once we identify a suitable company, we will share its profile, technical requirements, and a preliminary collaboration proposal. You can discuss the proposed role, workload, and remuneration with the company before deciding whether to apply. Any formal application would require your prior consent.

Please feel free to reach out if you have any other questions.

Best regards,
WuWei
Qingfei Tech Talent Team
```

**关键合规点**：
- 第 1 条严格采用「不公开宣传 + 有中文官方文件但不能发复印件 + 视频会议可展示 + 维基百科链接」；
- 维基百科 URL 单独成行、无 Markdown 包装、末尾无句号；
- 答疑结尾使用 `Please feel free to reach out if you have any other questions.`，**没有顺手索要 CV**；
- 署名沿用该线程原跟进账号 `WuWei`。

### 范例 2：首次人工回信 / 未回信温和跟进（简短正文 + 附 PDF）

**场景**：专家回复了首封触达邮件表示想了解更多，或前期沟通后未回信需附新版 `Qiming_Program_Information.pdf` 跟进。

```text
Dear Dr Ares,

I wanted to follow up on our earlier discussion about the relationship between solidification conditions and the mechanical and electrochemical properties of materials.

I have attached our updated program overview for your review. Would you still be interested in exploring potential collaboration with Chinese companies in this area?

Please feel free to reach out if you have any questions after reviewing the overview.

Best regards,
LiLei
Qingfei Tech Talent Team
```
*(提醒操作人在收发件箱挂载 `Qiming_Program_Information.pdf`)*

### 范例 3：专家已明确表达申报/匹配兴趣后的推进（索取 CV 阶段）

**场景**：专家已看过概览、没有未解疑问，明确表示愿意推进匹配企业。此时才进入索取 CV 阶段。

```text
Dear Dr [Last Name],

Thank you for your interest in exploring collaboration opportunities through the Qiming Program.

To help us identify Chinese companies whose technical needs align well with your research in [specific area], could you share an updated CV? If your CV includes any confidential project details, please feel free to omit them.

Once we identify suitable candidate companies, we will share their profiles and preliminary collaboration ideas with you before taking any further steps.

Best regards,
[Sender Name]
Qingfei Tech Talent Team
```

## 二、历史纠偏对照表（严禁重犯）

| 序号 | 曾出现的错误写法（反例） | 人工纠正后的标准做法（正例） | 原因说明 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 专家问官方文件时写：“I do not have a publicly available government notice or policy document for the current round to share. The PDF was prepared by our team...” | 统一写项目不公开宣传、我方持有中文官方政策文件但不能发送复印件、有意深入沟通可在视频会议中展示，并附维基百科纯链接。 | 旧写法显得我方缺少政策依据；实际是有中文官方文件但按规定不外发复印件。 |
| 2 | 专家来信提了 5 个问题，回信答完最后一段顺手加上：“If this sounds of interest, please send us your latest CV...” | 答疑邮件专注把问题答清楚，结尾统一写 `Please feel free to reach out if you have any other questions.`，不主动要 CV。 | 专家还在核实真实性与规则阶段，急于索要简历会增加戒备感。 |
| 3 | 在邮件草稿中输出 `see [Wikipedia’s article on the Thousand Talents Plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thousand_Talents_Plan).` | 引导语写 `see the Wikipedia article below.`，下一行空行后单独放原始 URL `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thousand_Talents_Plan`，无末尾句号。 | 纯文本拷贝到邮件编辑器时 Markdown 语法会原样裸露，且末尾句号易被误连进网址导致 404。 |
| 4 | 首封人工回信在正文里长篇罗列资助明细并直接索要 CV、学位证、护照。 | 首回采用「简短正文（结合研究方向）+ 附 `Qiming_Program_Information.pdf` + 确认探索意愿」。 | 项目详情已整合进标准 PDF 附件，首回过长且过早要证件会降低回复率。 |
| 5 | 把已知的“100 万个人补贴 + 200–400 万项目经费”模糊成“经费待定”，或反过来把待核实细项写成“我会联系当地人才办为您确认”。 | 已有确认 QA 的金额与条件准确复述；QA 未覆盖的细项先在对话里向用户请示口径。 | 既不能丢掉已有标准口径，也不能对外擅自作出额外行动承诺。 |
