### Deviations

1. **Two extra service methods, required by invariants whose behaviour the plan fixes but whose names it does not**: `renewLease(id, leaseToken, now)` (I-2 "worker每批续租"; CAS on `status='RUNNING' AND lease_token`, extends by 10 min) and `reopenFailed(id, now)` (I-3 "人工重试可显式重开 FAILED"; master 现状审计 item 6 "人工重试经同一 service"). The plan's three-function snippet names only `enqueue`/`claimDue`/`complete`; both additions are additive and leave those three names/shapes untouched. `reopenFailed` also resets `attempts` to 0 and clears `last_error` (a human retry must get a fresh failure budget, otherwise the very next transient failure re-lands `FAILED`); it keeps `result_json` as audit evidence.
2. **Merge/reopen column-level semantics** (plan fixes merge, re-open and the 30-day window but not the column detail): `PENDING`/`RUNNING`/`RETRY_WAIT`/`FAILED` merge without rewriting `source`, `discovery_execution_id`, `next_attempt_at` or `updated_at` (a repeated enqueue never creates a second row nor defers an already-scheduled pick-up, and the row keeps the execution that first produced it — asserted in the IT); `UNMATCHED` or a `SUCCEEDED` older than 30 days reopens to `PENDING` with `attempts = 0`, lease cleared, `last_error` cleared and `result_json` preserved as audit evidence.
3. **`Deferred.resetAt` is converted with `ZoneId.systemDefault()`** when written to the naive `DATETIME` column. The plan says "next_attempt_at 按 reset" and master I-5 says the real UTC reset must resume the work; the worker's `now` is a local-clock `LocalDateTime`, so labelling the reset in UTC wall time would have made the row due ~8h early on this JVM (Asia/Shanghai) and re-hit the quota. Conversion preserves the exact instant.
4. **`expert_doc_id` uses column-level `utf8mb4_bin`** (case-sensitive, V119 `folder` precedent). The plan fixes no collation; the default case-insensitive collation would let the unique key merge two byte-distinct ES `_id`s. Asserted in the Flyway test (`…009X` vs `…009x` → 2 rows).
5. **Storage-level details consistent with the repo's newest tables** (V119/V130 precedent, no behaviour change): `DATETIME(3)` instead of bare `DATETIME` (still `DATA_TYPE = datetime`), `DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP(3)` on `created_at`/`updated_at`, and a named `CHECK (status IN (…))` constraint (parsed/ignored on MySQL 5.7, enforced on 8.0.16+).
6. **`discovery_execution_id` has no foreign key** (the plan lists a plain nullable BIGINT): `task_execution` has retention cleanup, so an FK would block cleanup. Asserted (0 foreign keys on the table).
7. `SUCCEEDED`/`UNMATCHED` completions write `next_attempt_at = now` (no future schedule is implied by a terminal state); a claimed row keeps `last_error` until the next completion overwrites it.
8. Extra scoped evidence beyond the required command (in-scope-only scoped run, direct `node --test`) was needed only because the environment blocks the bare container command and surefire fails first; no product/test change depends on it.
